### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Senior Flutter & Mobile Developer** with **7+ years of commercial experience** architecting, engineering, and scaling robust cross-platform mobile solutions for **iOS and Android**. 

- 🏛️ **Architecture & Engineering Excellence**: Deep advocate for **Clean Architecture**, **SOLID principles**, and modular, feature-first package structures that scale cleanly across teams.
- 📱 **Native Capabilities & Channels**: Experienced in bridging Flutter with native **Android (Kotlin)** and **iOS (Swift)** platform channels for background processing, media streaming, VoIP/SIP, and biometric authentication.
- ⚡ **High Performance & Fluid UI**: Focused on 60fps rendering, memory optimization, custom canvas animations using `CustomPainter`, and zero-jank experiences.
- 🔄 **Continuous Delivery & Automation**: Streamlining mobile releases via **Fastlane**, **GitHub Actions**, and store automation across **App Store Connect** and **Google Play Console**.
- 🤝 **Cross-Functional Leadership**: Proven track record collaborating closely with Product Managers, UI/UX Designers (Figma), and QA teams to translate complex requirements into seamless digital products.
